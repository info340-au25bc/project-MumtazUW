Project Proposal: Simple Backlog Manager

The Problem with Modern Product Management Tools

Modern product management tools like Jira, Trello, and Azure DevOps offer features for tracking epics, user stories, backlogs, and sprints, but often assume a highly rigid workflow that doesn’t suit every team. They are also typically priced high enough to discourage small organizations from utilizing them altogether. These tools are often optimized for large-scale organizations with formalized agile practices, leaving smaller organizations, startups, hybrid teams, or non-profits struggling to adapt their processes to fit the tool rather than the other way around.

For example, a small nonprofit organization might juggle grant deadlines, grantee feedback, and fundraising organization alongside the development of an initiative or system. An early-stage startup may struggle to utilize expensive existing tools, thus relying on ineffective substitutes or forgoing the product management process altogether. These situations require a more flexible approach to backlog management and product development. Existing tools rarely support nuanced situations and tend to be overwhelming for users who simply want to track progress and collaborate across teams.

While existing platforms like Notion and Airtable can offer some customization, they typically lack the native support for product-specific concepts like epics, story points, and sprint management. This creates a gap for teams who need a lightweight, intuitive tool that still speaks the language of product management. Our app aims to fill this gap by offering a user-friendly, flexible system that can be tailored to teams who value adaptability and clarity!

App Description

Our web application, Simple Backlog Manager, allows users to create and organize work items for small projects. It is designed for anyone to use, with a focus on those who want to plan and track project tasks efficiently without relying on large, complex platforms.

Main Features

  • Adding, Editing, and Deleting Backlog Items: Users can create new tasks with a title, description, and priority level, as well as modify or remove existing ones.
  • Organizing and Filtering Tasks: Users can organize their items by status (To Do, In Progress, Done) and filter them by priority.
  • Persistent Storage (Small Feature): The application will use localStorage and Firebase to automatically save the backlog so users don’t lose data when they refresh or close the browser.
  • Search (Small Feature): Users can search for backlog items by title or description using a search input to quickly locate specific tasks.
