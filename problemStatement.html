The Problem with Modern Product Management Tools

Modern product management tools like Jira, Trello, and Azure offer features for tracking epics, user stories, backlogs, and sprints, but often assume a highly rigid workflow that doesnt suit every team. They are also typically priced high enough to discourage small organizations from utilizing them altogether. These tools are often optimized for large scale organizations with formalized agile practices, leaving smaller organizations, startups, hybrid teams, or non-profits struggling to adapt their processes to fit the tool rather than the other way around.

For example, a small nonprofit organization might juggle grant deadlines, grantee feedback, and fundraising organization alongside the development of an initiative or system. An early stage startup may struggle to utilize the expensive existing tools, thus relying on ineffective substitutes or forgoing the product management process altogether. These situations require a more flexible approach to backlog management and product development. Existing tools rarely support nuanced situations and tend to be overwhelming for users who simply want to track progress and collaborate across teams.

While existing platforms like Notion and Airtable can offer some more customization, they typically lack the native support for product-specific concepts like epics, story points, and sprint management. This creates a gap for teams who need lightweight , intuitive tools that still speak the language of product management. Our app aims to fill this gap by offering a user friendly, flexible system that can be tailored to teams who value adaptability and clarity!
